HOW TO STOP THE DEFI GUARDIAN DEMO
==================================

1. To stop the running servers:
   - Simply close the two black command windows:
     * "DeFi Guardian - Backend Server"
     * "DeFi Guardian - G2 Frontend"
   - Or click inside either command window and press Ctrl + C, then type Y and Enter.

2. To reset the paper trading account back to $10,000 simulated cash:
   - Go to Screen 7 (Settings) in the application and click "RESET TO $10K".
   - Or execute an HTTP POST to:
     http://127.0.0.1:8000/api/demo/reset

3. To relaunch the demo anytime:
   - Double-click START_DEMO.bat
