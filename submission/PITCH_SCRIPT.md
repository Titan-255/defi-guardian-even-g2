# DeFi Guardian — 90-Second Hackathon Pitch Script

*Duration: Approximately 75–90 seconds*  
*Tone: Confident, energetic, clear, authentic college builder*

---

### [0:00 - 0:15] The Hook & The Problem
"Hello judges! Crypto markets never sleep. Whether you're in class, walking to a meeting, or hanging out with friends, Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Solana are moving every second.

Right now, the only way to track that is pulling out your smartphone every 5 minutes, unlocking it, and staring at a tiny screen with a hundred flashing numbers. It breaks your focus, looks rude, and is completely overwhelming."

---

### [0:15 - 0:35] Introducing DeFi Guardian
"That's why I built **DeFi Guardian** — an AI-powered crypto intelligence companion built specifically for the Even Realities G2 smart glasses.

Instead of trying to cram an entire exchange onto a pair of glasses, DeFi Guardian delivers what actually matters, right in your peripheral field of view: live prices, local machine-learning anomaly detection, and instant paper trading with a single tap on the temple frame."

---

### [0:35 - 0:55] How It Works: Real Tech, Zero Gimmicks
"Under the hood, DeFi Guardian connects to Hyperliquid’s live API to stream real mid-prices.

Our local Python backend runs an **Isolation Forest** machine learning model directly on historical candle data. When Bitcoin or Solana experiences unusual trading volume or a sudden volatility spike, our ML engine detects it, calculates the statistical anomaly score, and explains exactly *why* in simple English: like 'Volume is 3.4 times higher than the recent 24-hour baseline.' No hallucinations, no external cloud AI fees—just real data science running locally."

---

### [0:55 - 1:15] The Read & Write Advantage: Wearable Paper Trading
"And we don't just read the market; we satisfy the hackathon's Read and Write requirement with a fully functional paper trading engine. 

With 10,000 simulated dollars, I can glance at a price on my glasses, preview a simulated 50-dollar buy order, and confirm it with a tap. The transaction commits atomically to SQLite, updating my portfolio in real time—completely risk-free."

---

### [1:15 - 1:30] Conclusion
"DeFi Guardian turns smart glasses from a passive notification screen into an active, hands-free financial intelligence tool.

Crypto intelligence, just a glance away. Thank you!"
